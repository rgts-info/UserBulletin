Using Odds Data for Horse Screening and Race Analysis
  • Navigate to the Odds Page: From the Race Selection Page (P12), press the "Odds" button. We will use Race 5 (Group 1) of the Epsom United Kingdom meeting on 06/06/2026 as our sample data.
  • Filter Win Odds: On the Odds page, press the "Win Trend" button (P13) to display only Win odds. Next, press the top "Select Odds" button twice (P14). The interface will initially show both Hong Kong World Pool and overseas data, but we want to focus on overseas win odds data first.
  • Identify Overseas Odds Columns: Scroll to the right and locate the "Overseas (Pa.Ring)" and "Overseas (Overnight)" columns (P15). The overseas odds shown here represent the best available odds (highest payout) consolidated from approximately 15 different bookmakers' data.
  • Understand the Time Slots: RGTS automatically records odds data across multiple key intervals (P16): Overnight, Parade Ring (Pa.Ring, roughly 5 to 6 minutes before the gates open), Last 3 Minutes, Last 1 Minute, and Current. Current live odds refresh automatically every 60 seconds during the final 10 minutes before the race starts.
  • Spot Market Support: Compare the "Overseas (Pa.Ring)" and "Overseas (Overnight)" columns. Look for horses whose Parade Ring odds are lower than their Overnight odds. In this sample race, five horses fit this criteria: 4, 5, 8, 13, and 14 (P17). These are the runners that warrant your attention.
  • Analyze the Local Favourite: Press the "Select Odds" button once more to reveal the "Overnight" column for the Hong Kong World Pool data, positioned between the two overseas columns. Check which horse is heavily backed in Hong Kong. For this race, horse number 7 was the Overnight favourite. However, comparing its Overseas Pa.Ring odds (3.7) to its Overseas Overnight odds (3.2) shows the price actually drifted higher at the parade ring (P18). This lack of strong late money suggests the favourite might be vulnerable, making this Group 1 race a prime opportunity to hunt for long-shots.
  • Evaluate Extreme Long-Shots: Reviewing the remaining short-listed runners (4, 5, 13, and 14), we see they are extreme long-shots with Parade Ring odds of 126-to-1, 81-to-1, 91-to-1, and 81-to-1 respectively. Insiders likely poured money into these massive prices because they recognized the favourite (7) was vulnerable—potentially due to the track condition changing to "Yielding to Soft." If you still strongly fancy the favourite (7) for exotic bets like Quinella (QIN), Quinella Place (QPL), or Forecast combinations, we advise treating it strictly as a "leg" rather than a "banker." For example, in a 1-Banker-3-Legs Quinella, place horse 7 among your legs, not as the single banker.
  • Track Live Fluctuations & Results: You can apply this same comparison technique between "Overseas (Overnight)" and "Overseas (Current)" odds at any time (P19). We highly recommend using the Parade Ring time slot as your primary baseline. From that point onward, monitor the 3-minute and 1-minute fluctuations. Looking at our shortlist (5, 8, 13, 14), it becomes obvious that horse number 8 attracted massive smart money, as both its 3-minute and 1-minute odds columns are highlighted in Green, indicating a price drop of 20% or more (P20). In fact, horse number 8 went on to win the race, paying out an official dividend of $65 HKD (P21)—a massive drop from its 20-to-1 Hong Kong overnight and 15-to-1 overseas overnight odds due to that heavy late betting volume.
  • Practice Makes Perfect: Please note that this strategy cannot be applied blindly to every single race. The more you practice this filtering technique using our "Past Race Day Reviews Page" (P22), the sharper your analysis will become. 運用賠率數據篩選馬匹和評估賽事形勢
  • 前往賠率頁面：喺「賽事選擇」頁面 (P12)，撳「賠率」掣。我哋會用 2026 年 6 月 6 日英國葉森打吡賽日嘅第 5 場一級賽作為示範數據。
  • 篩選獨贏賠率：入到賠率頁面後，撳「獨贏走勢」掣 (P13) 嚟只顯示獨贏賠率。然後撳兩次頂部嘅「選擇賠率」掣 (P14)。介面起初會同時顯示香港和海外賠率數據，但我哋想先將焦點擺喺海外嘅獨贏賠率數據上。
  • 尋找海外賠率欄目：向右拉，搵返「海外離沙圈」同「海外隔夜」呢兩個欄目 (P15)。介面顯示嘅海外賠率係匯集咗大約 15 間唔同外博公司嘅「最佳賠率（最高回報）」數據。
  • 了解數據時段：RGTS 會全自動記錄以下關鍵時段嘅賠率 (P16)：「隔夜」、「亮相圈/離沙圈（即起步前約 5 至 6 分鐘）」、「最後 3 分鐘」、「最後 1 分鐘」同「即時」。至於即時賠率，RGTS 喺開閘前最後 10 分鐘內，每 60 秒就會全自動更新一次。
  • 搵出初步落飛馬：對比「海外離沙圈」同「海外隔夜」兩欄，篩選出邊隻馬嘅臨場離沙圈賠率比隔夜賠率低。以呢場賽事為例，有5隻馬符合呢個條件：4號、5號、8號、13號、14號 (P17)。呢幾隻馬就係你需要高度留意嘅目標。
  • 分析本地大熱門：依家再撳一次「選擇賠率」掣，喺上述兩個海外欄目中間，會多出一個名為「隔夜」嘅欄目，呢個係香港嘅隔夜獨贏賠率。我哋可以睇吓邊隻係香港嘅大熱門。呢場隔夜大熱門係 7 號馬，但對比佢嘅海外離沙圈賠率 (3.7) 同海外隔夜賠率 (3.2)，會發現佢喺離沙圈時賠率反而變冷 (P18)。呢個現象通常代表隻大熱門臨場無強烈嘅資金支持。換句話講，呢場一級賽好有機會爆冷，非常適合向冷門馬埋手。
  • 評估超巨型冷門：再睇埋其餘4隻篩選出嚟嘅冷馬：4號, 5號、13號同14號。呢幾隻馬喺離沙圈時嘅賠率分別係 126倍、81倍、91倍同 81倍，屬於超級大冷門。有精明資金衝住呢幾隻大冷門而嚟，相信係因為有幕後知情人士睇穿咗大熱門 7 號並不穩陣——好大程度係因為當時跑道轉為「黏地至軟地」，而呢幾隻冷馬正好具備特佳嘅軟地性能。如果你依然好鍾意隻 7 號大熱門，如你打算投注連贏、位置Q、二重彩或其他組合，建議只宜將 7 號當作「腳」，千萬唔好用嚟做「單膽」。例如你要買一膽三腳嘅連贏，7 號應該擺喺三隻腳入面，而唔係做膽個隻。
  • 監察即時飛數變動及賽果：你隨時都可以用同樣嘅方法，去對比「海外隔夜」同「海外即時」賠率 (P19)。我哋強烈建議以「離沙圈」時段作為你嘅核心分析據點。由呢個時間點開始，密切監察最後 3 分鐘同 1 分鐘嘅飛數波動。睇返我哋手頭上嘅心水名單（4、5、8、13、14號），好明顯見到 8 號馬有極其強烈嘅精明資金湧入，因為佢嘅最後 3 分鐘同 1 分鐘賠率欄目都著咗「綠燈」，代表呢兩個時段嘅賠率都急跌咗 20% 或以上 (P20)。最終，呢隻 8 號馬不負眾望順利獨贏過終點，官方獨贏派彩為 $65 港元 (P21)！對比返佢原本喺香港隔夜有 20倍、海外隔夜有 15倍嘅落飛情況，正好印證咗臨場巨大投注量所帶來嘅威力。
  • 多用多練累積經驗：請記住，呢個篩選技巧並非套套皆準，絕不能盲目跟從。你越用得頻密、練得越多，預測就會越準。你可以善用我哋嘅「賽事重溫」 (P22) 嚟反覆練習。依家就建議你直接入去呢場示範賽事頁面，邊睇截圖邊對照一齊操作 (P23)。 💡 實戰小貼士：你可以撳下面個 「分享此教學」 掣，將個網址傳去你嘅電腦度開。用電腦大螢幕睇住教學，同時用手機開住個 App 嚟對照實操，練習效果會最好！
